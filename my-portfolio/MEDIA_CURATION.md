## 1. Executive Curation Philosophy & Visual Identity Protection

The portfolio preserves its distinct visual identity:
- **Canvas:** Warm off-white / ivory background (`#F7F5F0`)
- **Typography:** Bold engineering / editorial black typography (`#141822`)
- **Accents:** Metallic gold hairlines and badges (`#B8924A`, `#D4AF37`)
- **Engineered Visuals:** Hero-only PCB/circuit board network with orbital nodes
- **Hero Staging:** Dark 3D mechanical core with personal portrait cutout

Every image in the portfolio must:
1. **Earn its slot** by validating a specific technical claim (hardware built, code tested, competition ranked, leadership exercised).
2. **Harmonize** with the light editorial canvas without harsh visual clashes.
3. **Avoid redundancy**: Never reuse the same vehicle angle or team pose across different sections.
4. **Avoid the "Certificate Wall" trap**: Present formal credentials in an expandable, restrained micro-selector rather than flooding the layout with documents.
5. **Respect third-party attribution**: Explicitly attribute open-source and reference frameworks (such as VeRLab HeRo Common) without claiming personal authorship.
