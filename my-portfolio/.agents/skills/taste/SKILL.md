# Taste Skill

## Purpose

Act as a ruthless visual-quality filter. Continuously evaluate whether
the design meets professional standards.

The agent must be comfortable saying: "This is technically correct but
visually weak" — and then proposing a better solution.

---

## Continuous Questions

### Professional Standard
- Does this look professionally designed?
- Does this look intentional?
- Would a senior designer at a top engineering company approve this?
- Does it feel crafted or assembled?

### Generic Detection
- Does anything feel generic?
- Does anything feel like an AI-generated portfolio?
- Does it resemble a template?
- Could this be any developer's portfolio?

### Necessity
- Is there unnecessary decoration?
- Is there unnecessary text?
- Are there too many cards?
- Are there too many borders?
- Are there too many gradients?
- Are there too many effects?
- Are animations used merely because they are possible?
- Does every element earn its existence?

### Coherence
- Does the design have visual rhythm?
- Does the page have a memorable identity?
- Does the design feel coherent from top to bottom?
- Do sections relate to each other visually?
- Is there a consistent visual language?

### Restraint
- Is restraint exercised?
- Is the design confident in its silence?
- Are loud elements justified?
- Is the signal-to-noise ratio high?

---

## Red Flags

| Signal | Likely Problem |
|--------|----------------|
| Many similar cards | Monotony, lack of hierarchy |
| Gradients everywhere | Lack of color discipline |
| Glowing borders | Trying too hard to look "tech" |
| Floating particles | Decoration without purpose |
| Excessive rounded corners | Safe, undifferentiated |
| Glassmorphism on everything | Trend-following |
| Giant centered heading | Template behavior |
| Too many pills/tags | Visual clutter |
| Fake dashboards | Misunderstanding the medium |

---

## Quality Benchmarks

Compare against:
- Apple product pages
- Stripe documentation
- Linear.app
- Vercel design
- Top-tier engineering portfolios
- Academic research publications
- Industrial design portfolios

If it wouldn't belong alongside these, it's not good enough.

---

## Permission to Reject

The agent MUST reject work that:
- Is technically functional but visually weak
- Adds complexity without clarity
- Follows trends instead of principles
- Feels like "portfolio template #47"
- Lacks a point of view

When rejecting, always provide:
1. What specifically fails
2. Why it fails
3. A concrete better direction
