## Categories Created

1. **`achievements/certificates/`**:
   - Houses formal credentials and competition certificates (e.g. SAEINDIA aBAJA 2025 Certificate of Participation). Separates formal credentials from project engineering media.
2. **`activities/` (`anchoring/`, `campus-radio/`)**:
   - Groups extracurricular leadership roles (Anchoring Club Vice President, GITSwave Campus Radio Jockey) distinct from engineering projects.
3. **`hackathons/intel-ai/`**:
   - Dedicated space for hackathons that represent standalone competitive events without an extensive multi-artifact repository footprint.
4. **`internships/surgical-robotics/`**:
   - Distinguishes professional clinical robotics internship media (Versius surgical robot console, manipulator arms, scrubs) from collegiate vehicular and software projects.
5. **`projects/baja/baja-2025/` and `baja-2026/`**:
   - Separates the two distinct collegiate campaign years (2025 AIR-7 Car A12 vs 2026 AIR-11 Car A18) with subfolders for `vehicle`, `electrical`, and `team`.
6. **`projects/railguard-ai/`**:
   - Associates all media from the Faraway International Hackathon with the actual product developed (RailGuard AI: autonomous track inspection rover, mobile UI, and LiDAR point cloud).
